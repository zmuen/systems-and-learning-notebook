# What is network science
## Trivial networks vs. complex networks
Trivial networks: regular or random networks

## Network centrality
Given a network representation of a system, which are the most important modules or nodes? Or, which are the most important connections or edges?
There are several different metrics:
- nodes and edges that most pairs of other nodes communicate through them
- nodes and edges that, if removed, will cause the largest disruption in the underlying network
