## Power-law degree distribution

In practice, the degree distribution may not follow an ideal power-law distribution throughout the eintire ange of degrees.

For lower k, we observe a "lower-degree" saturation that decreases the probability of seeing low-edgree nodes compared to an ideal power-law. We can consider a modified power-law expression: $(k + k_{sat})^{-\alpha}$

For very high k, we often need to constraintthe upper-tail of the degree distibution up to a value of $k_{cut}$
